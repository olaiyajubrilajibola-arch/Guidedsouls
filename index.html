Before you begin

This test has 41 questions and lasts 1 hour 30 minutes. The clock starts when you press the button below.

  • Choose or type your answer, then press Confirm Answer.
  • A confirmed answer is saved and locked. You cannot change it afterwards, so check before you confirm.
  • After you confirm, you will see whether your answer was right. The correct answer is not shown.
  • Read the story passage at any time — it stays beside the questions, or tap Read passage on a phone.
  • If the page closes, open it again and enter the same details. Your answers and time are kept.
